Brand guidelines · v1

VortexFlow

A hand of cards fanned around your pointer — one of them chosen. Everything in this identity is a still frame of that moment: the spiral at rest.

01 · The mark

Four cards. One chosen.

The mark is the product's radial hub reduced until it survives a browser tab: a broken ring of four wedge cards with the chosen card popped outward in living teal, always toward the upper right. The void in the middle is the pointer — never fill it.

Geometry
Wedge spans 74° · 62° · 92° · 68°, gaps 16°
The chosen card
Popped +3 units along its bisector at −54°, grown +1.4
Ring
Radii 15.5 → 30 of a 64-unit square, corner radius 3.6
Clear space & minimum
Half a wedge thickness on all sides · 16 px (favicon cut below 24 px)
02 · Lockup & wordmark

Serif voice, set once.

The wordmark is Literata SemiBold converted to outlines — the webfont cousin of the site's Iowan Old Style headlines. Pair it with the mark on the left, cap-height aligned. Don't letter-space it, don't rebuild it in another face, don't bold it further.

03 · Color

Paper, ink, and one signal.

The world is warm paper and soft ink; teal is the single signal color — the chosen card, the live ring, the primary button. The pastels belong to cards: tints in the product UI, the app icon, and illustration. Roughly 80% paper · 15% ink · 5% teal on any surface.

04 · Typography

Editorial serif over system sans.

Display — Iowan Old Style · Literata on the web
Every window and tab,
at your cursor or key.
font-family: "Iowan Old Style","Palatino Linotype",Palatino,Literata,Georgia,serif · weight 500–600 · tracking −0.01em
Text — the system voice

Interface and body copy stay in the platform's own hand — SF Pro on Apple hardware. The product lives inside macOS; the type should feel like it never left. Sentence case everywhere, including buttons.

font-family: -apple-system,"SF Pro Text",system-ui,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif · 15–17px body · 600 for labels
05 · The living mark

The logo is a still of this.

On the site, the identity moves: <vortex-spiral> renders the hub live — hover a card and it becomes the chosen one; scroll on the ring and it spins, the same flick as the app. It ships in the pack and always sits on a paper stage.

Idle orbit
3°/s, eases to rest while the pointer is inside
The pop
+3.2 units · scale 1.03 · spring cubic-bezier(.3,1.4,.4,1)
Hub ring
Takes the chosen card's hue; glow stays soft
Respect
No motion under prefers-reduced-motion
06 · App icon & social

Color lives in the dock.

The macOS icon is the one place the pastel colorway leads — a full ring of card colors on a paper squircle, chosen card still teal, still upper-right. The OG card keeps the site's editorial voice with the soft-pastel ring bleeding off the edge.
