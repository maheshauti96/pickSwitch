Privacy

An account. Not your windows.

Last updated: 28 August 2026

You create an account to start the limited-time free preview. We keep what that account needs — typically an email and a way to sign in. We do not take the contents of your windows, your screenshots, or what you type into the overlay as analytics.

The short version

  • An account is required. No credit card is required to start the preview.
  • Account data is used to run the preview and, if you buy later, the license.
  • Window titles, search queries, and thumbnails stay on your Mac. They are not a product analytics stream.
  • The app still fetches site icons as described below. Never for a private window.

Your account

When you create an account we store the details you give us (email, and a password or sign-in token) so we can recognise you, start the preview, and later attach a paid license if you choose one. We do not sell this. We do not use it to advertise.

The favicon network call

To show the icon of a site open in your browser, VortexFlow fetches that icon from that site’s own address. The request is unauthenticated. It sends no cookies and nothing identifying. It is never made for an incognito or private-browsing window. If you deny the app network access, cards still work; they just will not show site icons.

What the permissions can see

macOS will ask. Each permission is optional. The app runs with any subset. Details: Permissions.

  • Input Monitoring — which mouse button you pressed, so the overlay can open. Not a keylogger; it does not record or transmit what you type.
  • Accessibility — the list of windows, and raising the exact one you pick. Without it, whole apps come forward instead of specific windows.
  • Screen Recording — window thumbnails, captured on demand when the overlay is up. They are not saved and not sent as telemetry. Icon View needs none of this.
  • Automation — asked later, the first time tab search or site icons need it. Used to read tab titles and URLs from browsers you use, on your machine.

What we never touch

  • Keychain items that are not our own sign-in, clipboard, contacts, calendar, location
  • The files on your disk

This website

vortexflow.io is a static site. It does not set marketing cookies. If the host provides privacy-preserving request analytics with no cookies, that applies to the website only.
