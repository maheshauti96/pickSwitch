When a build is ready — Gatekeeper

VortexFlow is signed, but it is not notarized, because notarizing requires a paid Apple Developer account. macOS may refuse to open an unnotarized app and say the developer cannot be verified. That is expected.

  1. Open the disk image and drag VortexFlow to your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS will refuse. Leave that dialog.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway next to VortexFlow. Authenticate when asked.
  4. Grant the permissions it asks for, then create an account. You only fight Gatekeeper once.

On macOS 15 and later, right-clicking the app and choosing Open no longer works as a shortcut for this. Apple removed that bypass. See Apple’s own documentation.
